# Three Names for the Warehouse District

![Three Names for the Warehouse District](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Warehouse_district_street.jpg)

The tourism map says Foundry Quarter. The bus map says South Depot. The delivery drivers still say Warehouse District. All three names are true in different economies.

The field atlas now lists the names together instead of choosing one. That is not indecision; it is a warning that a search box may fail depending on who gave the directions.

A good map records conflict when the conflict helps someone navigate.
