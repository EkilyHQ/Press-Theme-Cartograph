# Market Lane Detour Card

![Market Lane Detour Card](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Street_market_map.jpg)

The official detour card named the closed street first. The better card names the open path first. Closures matter to agencies; routes matter to walkers.

We tested three versions with vendors before the morning rush. The card with landmarks won: flower stall, blue awning, library steps, east gate.

A map succeeds when a person can ignore most of it and still arrive.
