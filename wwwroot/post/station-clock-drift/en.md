# Station Clock Drift

![Station Clock Drift](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Railway_station_clock.jpg)

The east clock runs two minutes fast, which is harmless until the west platform announces final boarding on time. Then everyone on the footbridge starts walking faster than the signage expects.

I timed the crossing six times. The problem is not speed; it is confidence. People trust the visible clock more than the speaker.

The atlas correction recommends aligning the clocks before rewriting the signs. Infrastructure often needs maintenance before messaging.
