# Canal Mile Zero

![Canal Mile Zero](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Canal_mile_marker.jpg)

Mile zero is less a point than a civic agreement. People use it to describe walks, repairs, races, and where the ice usually breaks first.

The marker is chipped, but the directions around it are precise. That is often how field geography works: the object fades while the shared reference strengthens.

Cartograph gives this post the right weight: enough structure for a dispatch, enough warmth for local memory.
