# A Daybook for an Outline Map

![A Daybook for an Outline Map](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/United_States_Public_Domain_Map.svg)

An outline map is useful because it refuses to finish the thought. It leaves room for weather, delays, errands, and the private legends that no official atlas can hold.

This week the blank space filled with three calls, one bridge closure, two names for the same hill, and a note that the diner at mile 84 now opens at six instead of five-thirty.

For demo content, an outline also tests contrast. The map remains readable beside Cartograph's paper tones, and the source note does not compete with the route.

A good daybook entry is not comprehensive. It is specific enough that the next person can decide whether to trust yesterday's map.
