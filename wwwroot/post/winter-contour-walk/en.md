# Winter Contour Walk

![Winter Contour Walk](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Contour_map.jpg)

Snow simplified the hill. Footprints showed the practical contour, not the surveyed one, and the practical line was kinder to knees.

We added a winter variant to the route card: stay below the oak line until the second bench, then climb diagonally toward the ridge marker.

Seasonal maps are not clutter if the season changes the decision. In this case, winter deserves its own line.
