# Night Bus Transfer at Willow Loop

![Night Bus Transfer at Willow Loop](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Night_bus_stop.jpg)

Willow Loop looks simple on the schedule and confusing on the pavement. The two shelters face different roads, and the useful sign is behind the driver once the bus arrives.

At night the error becomes visible because everyone waits under the brighter lamp, even when that is the wrong side for the return trip.

The atlas note now leads with the lamp, then the shelter, then the route number. That is the order a tired passenger can use.
