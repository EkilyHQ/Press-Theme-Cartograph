# About Nora Quill Field Atlas

Nora Quill Field Atlas is a fictional independent field publication about routes and the ordinary systems that help people move: ferry timetables, map drawers, trail junctions, station clocks, and civic diagrams. It is written as if maintained by a working route researcher with a small archive and a habit of publishing dispatches after each trip. All field locations, assignments, and people are invented for the demo; the source images are public-domain or CC0-compatible materials cited inside the articles.

This demo uses the `cartograph` theme and fictional editorial content generated for theme testing.
